Parent and Guardian Guide

The game is a simple car-dodging game with non-graphic crashes. It has no chat, public profile, wager, prize or cash-out in the reviewed version.

Can a minor play?

Yes, with parent or guardian permission, if the adult considers the content suitable for the child. Encourage breaks and supervise younger children.

Can a minor pay?

No. Only an adult aged 18 or older may be the buyer. The adult must make the purchase in the adult's own name and use a payment method the adult is authorised to use.

Purchase safety

  • Passes are fixed and do not renew.
  • The free restart remains available.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN, password or full card number with the game or support.
  • Ask for a refund if a child made an unauthorised direct purchase.

Privacy

No account or advertising system is present. If child personal data is introduced later, the service must use verifiable parental consent where required and must not target ads or behaviourally track known children.
