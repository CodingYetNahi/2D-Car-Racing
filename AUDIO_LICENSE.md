# Engine audio

The racing engine sound used by RacingGame.fun is sourced from the public `clhforensics/indygp` asset set and ultimately from the OpenGameArt **racing car engine sound loops** by domasx2.

- Original source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
- License: CC0 / public domain dedication
- Runtime sample: `f1_rpm_step_2.wav` from the indygp public audio assets

The OpenGameArt page states that the loops were remade using a public-domain sample and lists the asset under CC0.
