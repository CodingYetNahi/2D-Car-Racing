Contact and Grievances

Payments are disabled. Before launch, this page must state the merchant's verified legal name, business address, private support email, grievance contact and response targets. The backend also refuses to create orders unless these launch settings exist.

For a current technical bug, use the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post personal information, payment references, bank details, card details, OTPs, passwords or identity documents in a public issue.

Payment, privacy and child-safety complaints will use a private channel before payments are enabled.
