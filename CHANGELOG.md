# Changelog

All notable releases of RacingGame.fun will be recorded here.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-15

Initial formally versioned release of RacingGame.fun.

This baseline includes the browser-based three-lane racing game, mobile and keyboard steering, traffic and collision systems, scoring, pass/payment flow, car skins, verified-score infrastructure, responsive mobile layout, and engine audio support.

Versioning follows semantic versioning from this release forward:
- Patch (`1.0.x`) — backward-compatible bug fixes.
- Minor (`1.x.0`) — backward-compatible features or substantial improvements.
- Major (`x.0.0`) — incompatible or fundamental changes.
